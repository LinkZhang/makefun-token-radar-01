How the model works

One formula scores every row, from the snapshot's own figures only. No protocol name is ever special-cased.

What the board is not

The board does not predict whether or when a protocol issues a token, and an implied value is this entry's claim, never a market price. A protocol the snapshot records as having no token may still have one its record does not carry. Farm steps are a starting plan, not advice, and every step names what it costs before you spend it.
